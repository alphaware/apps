Common questions

Does editing replace my original photos?

No. iLovePhoto imports a copy and creates new files for every export. Your originals in Photos or Files are never overwritten.

How do I save or share an edited photo?

Choose a tool, select your photos, adjust the settings and tap Export. On the result screen, use Share or save to Files, or Save images to Photos. You can reopen generated files from Exports.

Why can't I save to Photos?

Allow iLovePhoto to add photos in your iPhone or iPad Settings. If you prefer not to grant access, use Share or save to Files instead. PDFs and text files can be saved to Files.

Can I use iLovePhoto offline?

Yes, once your selected source files are available on the device. Importing a photo stored only in iCloud or another cloud service may require an internet connection to download it first.

Which file formats and batch sizes are supported?

You can import up to 30 images per batch, up to 100 MB per file, using formats supported by iOS ImageIO. Exports include JPG, PNG, HEIC and TIFF, plus collage, GIF, PDF and text tools. Animated images, Live Photos and multi-page images are edited as still images using the first frame. SVG and PSD are not supported.

Why did the output size or file size change?

JPG and HEIC compression can lose detail, and some inputs produce larger files after conversion. Source processing is limited to 24 megapixels and 6000 pixels on the longest side; GIF frames are limited to 800 pixels. Resize fits inside your selected dimensions while preserving aspect ratio. Enlargement interpolates pixels and isn't AI detail recovery.

How do I keep a transparent background?

After removing the background, choose PNG or TIFF and leave White background instead of transparency off. JPG and HEIC exports place transparent pixels on white.

Did face blur hide every face?

Not necessarily. Automatic face detection can miss faces, and background removal and text recognition can make mistakes. Inspect every result before sharing, especially when privacy matters.

How do I keep editing an exported photo?

Open its result screen and choose Edit this result, then pick the next tool. The previous export remains available.
