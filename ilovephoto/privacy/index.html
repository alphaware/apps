iLovePhoto

Privacy policy

iLovePhoto · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

iLovePhoto doesn't collect or transmit your photos or personal information. Photo editing, background removal, face detection and text recognition run on your device. There's no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or external image-processing service.

Photos and Files

You select images through Apple's Photos and Files pickers. iLovePhoto stores imported copies and generated files in its private documents folder. Files selected from iCloud or another cloud provider may need to download through that provider before import; the provider's own privacy policy applies.

Saving and device backups

Saving an image to Photos requests permission to add items to your photo library. You can deny this permission and still share exports to Files. Depending on your iOS settings, system device backups may include iLovePhoto's local documents.

Image metadata

Generated image exports are re-encoded without copying the source GPS, EXIF, camera or capture-date dictionaries. Original imported copies may still contain that metadata. Removing metadata doesn't remove information visible in the picture, such as text, faces or landmarks.

Text recognition and clipboard

Text recognition uses Apple Vision on your device. Extracted text is saved locally. It is copied to the system clipboard only when you tap Copy text.

Sharing

Exports leave the app only when you choose a sharing destination or save them to Photos or Files. The destination's own privacy policy applies. If you email support and include an attachment, you choose which information to send.

Deleting your data

Delete an imported photo from the app's Photos tab to remove the local copy, or delete a batch from Exports to remove its generated files. Your original items in Photos or Files are kept. Uninstalling iLovePhoto removes its local container; copies you saved elsewhere and existing device backups are managed separately.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
