Oftenly

Privacy policy

Oftenly · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

Oftenly has no account system, developer-operated servers, advertising, analytics or tracking. We cannot see your habits. Your habit data is kept on your device and, when iCloud sync is enabled, in your private iCloud account.

What's stored on your device

Oftenly stores the habits you create, schedules, goals, completion history, notes, moods, timer entries and preferences in its local app storage. iOS may include this storage in your own device or computer backups, depending on your settings.

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync and backup is enabled, Oftenly stores habit records in your private iCloud storage through Apple's system service. Those records can include habit names, schedules, goals, notes and completion history. Apple handles that service under its own policies; Oftenly's developer does not receive a copy. You can turn sync off in Oftenly's Settings. Turning it off does not itself erase records already stored in iCloud.

Apple Health

With your permission, Oftenly reads the Health categories you choose, such as steps, exercise, mindfulness or sleep, to complete matching habit goals. It never writes to Apple Health. Raw Health readings are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to our servers. Resulting habit goals and check-ins are part of your habit history and may be included in your private iCloud sync or a backup you export.

Widgets, Shortcuts and Apple Watch

Oftenly shares habit information with its own widgets and system actions using app-specific shared storage on your device. If you use the companion Apple Watch app, today's habit information and check-ins are exchanged with your paired watch using Apple's connectivity service.

Reminders and app lock

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS after you grant notification permission. If you enable app lock, Apple's authentication system checks Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Oftenly does not receive your biometric information.

Exporting and sharing

Export Data creates a file containing your habits and history only when you ask. You decide where to save or share it, and the destination's privacy policy applies. Imported backups are read from the files you choose. If you email support, you choose which information or attachments to include.

Deleting your data

Deleting a habit in Oftenly removes its local history; when iCloud sync is enabled, that deletion is also sent to your private iCloud storage. Archiving keeps the habit and its history. Uninstalling the app removes its local container but does not necessarily erase iCloud records, files you exported or existing device backups. Those copies are managed separately through the relevant Apple settings or storage destination.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
