Common questions

Was Oftenly previously called Habitly?

Yes. Oftenly is the app's current name. Some older backup files and internal identifiers may still use Habitly; you can use Settings → Import Data to restore compatible backups.

How do I track a habit?

Create a habit, choose its schedule, and tap its card when you've completed it. Open the habit's detail view to see its history or update a past due day.

Do days off break my streak?

No. Streaks count consecutive days when the habit is due and skip unscheduled days. Today stays pending until the day is over, so an unfinished habit does not immediately break your streak.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

With iCloud sync enabled and the same Apple Account, Oftenly can sync your saved habits. You can also use Settings → Export Data on the old phone, keep the JSON file somewhere safe, then use Settings → Import Data on the new phone. Imports merge compatible history rather than deleting your existing habits.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that the habit has a reminder enabled and that Oftenly has notification permission in iPhone Settings. Focus and notification settings may affect alerts. iOS limits how many reminders an app can schedule.

Can I use Oftenly without iCloud?

Yes. Habit tracking works locally. Turn off iCloud sync and backup in Oftenly's Settings if you want to keep using it only on this device, and export a backup before changing phones or uninstalling.

Why didn't my Apple Health habit complete?

Check that you've linked the habit to the correct Health category, allowed read access and reached its goal. Open Oftenly after the Health information becomes available. Oftenly reads only the supported categories you choose and does not write to Health.

What's the difference between archiving and deleting?

Archiving removes a habit from your active routine while keeping its history, and you can restore it from Settings → Archived habits. Deleting removes that habit and its history.

Where do I find backups and appearance settings?

Open Settings in Oftenly. You can change appearance, manage archived habits, configure iCloud sync, and export or import your data there.
