Privacy policy

Ludo Lounge · Last updated 10 November 2026

Who operates the app

Ludo Lounge is provided by Yawar Azad, publishing as Alphaware. Contact: jkexam.in@gmail.com. This policy covers the iPhone app and the Ludo service at ludo.jkexam.in.

Local play

Local matches, preferences, virtual coins and cosmetic progression are stored on your iPhone. They are not a server-owned wallet and have no cash value. Device backups may include local app storage under Apple's backup settings.

Online guest players

Online play creates a device-local guest identity. The service receives your chosen display name, a random player identifier, room membership, readiness and game actions. A bearer credential is kept in iOS Keychain; the server stores its hash rather than the credential. These records are linked to your guest identifier for game functionality and security. No Apple Game Center login, email or real name is required.

Chat and safety

Messages are optional and shared with players at your table. Chosen names and chat are filtered for a first line of protection. Reports include the reporter and reported guest identifiers, table identifier, reason, display name and selected message when applicable. Blocking records the two guest identifiers to hide communication and prevent future matching. Moderators can review reports and suspend a guest. Filtering cannot detect every form or language of abuse.

Computer opponents

Play Now first looks for human players. After eight seconds, server-controlled computer players fill empty seats; they are labelled Computer. A creator can start with computers immediately. Computer opponents are automated game logic, not human players or a generative AI chat service.

Network and operational data

The service processes network information such as IP addresses for requests, rate limiting and security. Operational hosting/access logs may contain IP addresses and request metadata. We do not use these logs to build advertising profiles. Our hosting and network providers process the data needed to deliver and protect the service.

Advertising

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads may be enabled by the service. Requests are configured for non-personalized ads, with consent where required, and the app does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. Non-personalized does not mean no data collection. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location including IP-derived location, ad impressions/interactions, product interactions, crash logs and performance data for advertising, analytics and SDK operation. Some data may be associated with a device. The app does not send Google your lounge name, chat or guest credential, and does not intentionally combine lounge records with advertising data. Google's policies and your consent choices also apply.

Choices

Where required, Google presents consent choices before ads can load. Settings shows Ad privacy choices when the consent framework makes those choices available. You can play local games offline. Chat is optional; use mute, block and report controls for unwanted communication.

Retention

Guest credentials expire after 30 days. Active rooms expire within 24 hours; finished rooms usually expire within one hour. The service prunes expired data periodically. Retry receipts expire after a day. Safety reports may be retained for up to 30 days, including relevant reported content after a guest is deleted, to investigate abuse. Blocks last while the corresponding guest identities exist. Operational records are kept only as needed for security and service operation.

Deletion

Use Play online → Delete my online player to delete your guest credential/session, remove your chat and anonymize your roster name in retained tables. Leaving a table does not delete the guest. Delete the app to remove ordinary local saves and settings; to delete the online guest, use the in-app deletion control before uninstalling. iOS Keychain credentials can outlast uninstalling. Report evidence may remain for the limited safety-retention period above. Contact support for privacy questions; losing a device credential can prevent verification or recovery of an old seat.

Support

If you email support, we receive the email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. Do not send passwords, guest credentials, payment details or sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is used to respond and resolve the issue.

Age and changes

Online play is intended for ages 13 and over. The app is not a children's advertising service. If you believe a child has provided inappropriate personal information, contact support. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
