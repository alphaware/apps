Common questions

How do I start quickly?

Open Play online, enter a friendly name and tap Play Now. We look for humans for eight seconds, then labelled computers fill open seats. Or choose Play vs computer from the home screen for offline play.

How do I play with friends?

Create a private room and share its six-character code. Everyone marks ready; the creator starts the game. Online tables require internet.

How do I report or block someone?

Open table chat and its history/player controls. Use Players → Report or Block, or the flag beside a message. Reporting also blocks that guest. Reports are available to moderators. For a follow-up, email support with the report reference; avoid including private credentials.

What happens if I leave?

On updated tables an explicit departure forfeits your seat. Temporary inactivity uses automatic actions, but three missed turns cause a loss. A manual accepted game action resets missed turns.

Can coins be bought or redeemed?

No. They are local cosmetic rewards. There are no coin purchases, wagers, entry fees, cash prizes or redemption.

How do I change my name or delete my player?

Open Play online to change your name or choose Delete my online player. Local game names can be edited from table options. Online deletion does not erase local saves.
