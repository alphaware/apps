Community rules

Play respectfully

Online play is for ages 13+. No hateful, sexual, threatening, abusive or harassing names or messages. Do not promote illegal activity, spam, impersonation or scams. Do not share private contact information or links in chat.

Know your opponent

Computer opponents are automated and labelled Computer. Human-player availability varies. Do not impersonate the developer, another player or a moderator.

Report and block

Use the message flag or Players → Report in chat history. Reporting records relevant content and blocks that guest from your chat and future matching. You can also block without reporting. For urgent follow-up or a report outside the app, email jkexam.in@gmail.com with the reference, display name, room code and approximate time. Never include guest credentials.

Moderation

The service filters names and messages, retains reports in a private moderation queue, and allows the operator to suspend abusive guest identities. Reports are reviewed to decide appropriate action; malicious reports are not permitted. Filters are imperfect: block and report anything that gets through. Moderation contact: jkexam.in@gmail.com.

Fair play

Do not exploit the service, automate abuse, manipulate reports or interfere with another player's connection. The server owns online dice and game state. Virtual coins and the local tournament do not involve money, stakes or prizes.
