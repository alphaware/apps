Paperly

Privacy policy

Paperly · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

Paperly doesn't collect any data. Scanning, OCR, compression and every other tool run on your device. There's no account, no document upload server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive your documents.

Documents and exports

PDFs, extracted text and generated images are stored in Paperly's private library on your device. Passwords you enter to open, protect or unlock a PDF are used only for that operation and aren't saved. A signature you draw is used only for the document you're signing.

Camera, Photos and Files

The camera is used only while you scan. Apple's Photos picker gives Paperly only the images you choose. If you save page images to Photos, Paperly asks for add-only access, which can't read your library. The Files picker gives access only to the files you select. Importing from iCloud or another provider may download through that provider, whose own policy applies.

Sharing and backups

Files leave Paperly only when you share, print or export them, and go only where you choose; the destination's own policy then applies. Depending on your settings, iCloud or computer backups may include Paperly's library.

Deleting your data

Delete a document or export in the app to remove that copy, or delete the app to remove everything Paperly stored. Copies you shared or saved elsewhere are managed there.

Children

Paperly isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
