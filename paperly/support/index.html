Common questions

How do I scan a document?

Tap Scan a document. Hold your iPhone over the page; Paperly finds the edges and captures automatically, or tap the shutter. When you're done, review the pages: rename the scan, choose a filter, rotate, reorder or add pages, then tap Save.

Can I add pages to a scan later?

While reviewing a scan, tap + to scan more pages or add photos. For a saved document, use Merge to combine it with another PDF, or Organize to change page order.

Why is my compressed PDF not smaller?

Some PDFs are already efficient. Paperly never gives you a file larger than the original and tells you when it can't reach your target size. Compression flattens pages, so searchable text, links and forms may be removed.

Is the signature legally binding?

Paperly adds a drawn, visual signature. It isn't a certificate-based digital signature. Check what the recipient accepts.

Does Paperly work offline?

Yes. Every tool runs on your device. You need a connection only to fetch files stored in a cloud provider or to share somewhere online.

Where are my files stored?

In Paperly's library on your device. Use Share, Print or Save to Files to put a copy somewhere else.
