Launchloom

Privacy policy

Launchloom · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

Launchloom doesn't collect any data. Your projects, screenshots and exports stay on your device. There's no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Photos

When you use Import from Photos, you choose the images in Apple's picker. When you start a capture session, Launchloom asks for access to your photo library so it can list the screenshots you take after tapping Start. It reads only screenshots taken during the session, and never uploads or shares them.

Screen recording

If you record your screen, Launchloom's recording extension runs on your device, keeps still frames when the screen settles, and saves them to storage shared only with Launchloom. It doesn't record audio, and nothing is uploaded. You can stop recording at any time from the red indicator.

Files and exports

Files you import are copied into Launchloom's private storage. Exports are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them; the destination's own privacy policy then applies.

Deleting your data

Delete a project from Studio, or delete the app to remove everything Launchloom stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
