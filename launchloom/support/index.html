Common questions

Can Launchloom open my other apps?

No. iOS doesn't let any app open or list other apps. Start a capture session or a screen recording in Launchloom, switch to your app yourself, then come back.

Why are some screenshots missing in a capture session?

If you allowed access to only some photos, new screenshots aren't visible to Launchloom. Allow access to all photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Launchloom.

Does Launchloom upload to App Store Connect?

No. Share the ZIP or files to your Mac, then upload them in App Store Connect or Google Play Console.

Which sizes do I need?

Apple requires a 6.9-inch iPhone set, and a 13-inch iPad set if your app runs on iPad. Launchloom's Guide tab links to Apple's and Google's current specifications.
