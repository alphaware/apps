Softly

Privacy policy

Softly · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

Softly doesn't collect any data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests.

What's stored, and where

Your logs (period days, symptoms, moods, notes, temperatures and other entries) and your settings are saved only in Softly's private storage on your iPhone. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on in your iPhone's settings, iOS may include this storage in your device backup, which is protected by Apple's own backup security.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They're worded discreetly and never contain your health details.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face or passcode on the device. Softly never receives biometric data.

Exporting and sharing

Backups and doctor reports are created only when you ask, and go only where you choose to share them. Once shared, the destination's own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use My cycle → Privacy & backup → Delete all my data, or delete the app. Either removes everything Softly stored on your iPhone.

Health information

Predictions are estimates based on what you log. Softly isn't birth control, doesn't diagnose any condition and isn't a substitute for medical advice.

Children

Softly isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Because Softly collects nothing, a change could never apply to data already collected.
