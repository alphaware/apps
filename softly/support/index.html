Common questions

How does Softly predict my period?

From the periods you log. With no history it starts from the cycle and period lengths you entered, then uses your own recent cycles. The likely range narrows as you log more.

Can I use Softly as birth control?

No. Fertile days are an estimate and can be wrong. Please don't rely on Softly to prevent pregnancy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Use My cycle → Privacy & backup → Back up to Files, then restore that file on the new iPhone. A full iPhone backup also includes Softly.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings → Notifications → Softly, and that the reminder is on in My cycle → Reminders.

My cycles are irregular.

Softly shows a wider likely range when your cycles vary. If your cycle changes a lot or something worries you, talk to a doctor.
